FINRA Rule 4210 Update

Future Roadmap: June 2026 Deployment

The SEC and FINRA have approved major amendments to Rule 4210, marking a historic shift in how margin accounts are regulated. These changes are designed to modernize trading for the real-time era.

1. Elimination of the PDT Designation

The long-standing "Pattern Day Trader" designation is being retired. Broker-dealers will no longer be required to track if a client makes 4 or more trades in a 5-day period.

  • Old Rule: 4 trades in 5 days = Restricted.
  • New Rule: Trading frequency is no longer a restricted metric.

2. Removal of the $25,000 Equity Requirement

Perhaps the most significant change for retail traders is the removal of the $25,000 minimum equity threshold specifically for day traders.

  • The minimum equity will now align with standard margin requirements (typically $2,000).

3. Real-Time Intraday Buying Power

Buying power will now update dynamically based on real-time margin excess rather than prior-day closing prices. This provides a much clearer view of what an account can support as market conditions change.
